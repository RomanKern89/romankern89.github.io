Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

This policy describes how the YouTube publishing tool operated by Roman Kern (the "tool", see the home page) handles data obtained through Google APIs.

Who uses the tool

The tool is used only by its author to manage his own YouTube channels. It is not offered to other people, and no other person can sign in to it.

Data accessed through Google APIs

  • YouTube Data API — the author's own channel information, the list and metadata of the author's own videos; uploading videos, thumbnails and metadata to the author's channels; changing the privacy status of the author's own videos.
  • YouTube Analytics API — read-only statistics of the author's own channels and videos.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish the author's videos and to evaluate how they perform. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not transferred to third parties and not used to train machine-learning models.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens and retrieved statistics are stored only on the author's own computer, in a directory accessible to the author's user account. Nothing is stored on third-party servers.

Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation the stored token stops working and is deleted from the author's computer; locally kept statistics are deleted on request.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
