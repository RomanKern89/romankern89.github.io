Roman Kern — YouTube publishing tool

A private tool that publishes technical videos to the author's own YouTube channels.

What it is

This is a personal automation tool used by Roman Kern to prepare and publish IT videos to his own two YouTube channels: the Russian-language channel @roman.kern1 and the English-language channel @itweight. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does with YouTube

  • uploads videos, thumbnails, titles, descriptions and tags to the author's channels;
  • changes the privacy status of the author's own videos when they are ready;
  • reads the author's own channel and video statistics (YouTube Analytics) to evaluate results.

It does not access anyone else's channel, does not post on behalf of other people and does not collect data about viewers.
